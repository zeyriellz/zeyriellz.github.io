# Nety Sulistyorini — Synthwave / Cyberpunk High-Tech Developer Portfolio

A modern, luxurious, and fully responsive personal portfolio website crafted for **Nety Sulistyorini** (Information Systems Business Student | Aspiring Business & System Analyst at State Polytechnic of Malang), styled in a futuristic **Synthwave / Cyberpunk High-Tech** aesthetic.

---

## 🎨 Synthwave / Cyberpunk Color Palette

- **Main Background**: `#1A0B2E` (Deep Midnight Blue-Purple, almost black)
- **Glassmorphism Cards**: `#3F194D` (Dark Purple with 60% opacity & `backdrop-blur-xl`)
- **Primary Accents**: `#C91C7A` (Neon Magenta) & `#FFCA06` (Neon Yellow / Cyber Gold)
- **Secondary Accents**: `#68097E` (Bright Purple) & `#FFA726` (Cyber Amber)
- **Typography & Paragraphs**: Crisp off-white & light slate (`#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`, `#D8B4E2`) ensuring maximum readability and contrast.
